> Mentor Spaces is a structured mentorship platform that helps nonprofits, universities, and companies launch, manage, and measure mentorship programs that unlock funding, improve career outcomes, and prove measurable impact.

## What we do

Mentor Spaces provides software for running structured mentorship programs at scale. Our platform handles automated mentor-mentee matching, scheduling, communication, and real-time outcome tracking. Customers use Mentor Spaces to:

- Secure and renew grant funding by generating funder-ready impact reports
- Increase student persistence, graduation, internship placement, and post-graduation employment
- Engage alumni in time-bound, structured mentorship rather than open-ended commitments
- Run skills-based volunteering and corporate mentorship programs that generate measurable CSR/ESG outcomes
- Scale programs without burning out staff

## Audiences

- **Nonprofits**: youth mentoring, college access, workforce readiness, reentry, professional development.
- **Universities**: career mentoring, peer mentoring, alumni-to-student, first-generation student support, identity-based affinity programs.
- **Companies**: skills-based volunteering, ERGs, internal employee mentorship, leadership development, CSR programs.

## Pricing

- Launch: $8 per participant per month, up to 100 participants.
- Grow: $6 per participant per month, up to 500 participants.
- Thrive: custom enterprise pricing for 500+ participants, includes SSO, API, custom integrations, and a dedicated success manager.

No long-term contracts. No setup fees.
